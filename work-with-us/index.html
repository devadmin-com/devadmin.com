Work With Us

Join Devadmin!

Join a globally distributed team working on high-performance systems serving millions of pages every day. We are distributed agile team focused on great collaboration. We use the latest tools and development technologies. We are a learning organisation following agile distributed team best practices.

→ See Open Jobs Annual Summits & Dev Camps
  • We build high-traffic sites, facing real-world challenges everyday
  • We are a non-hierarchical team with shared decision making so you participate in deciding teams technological development and process improvement
  • Agile – We are an agile team with a regular sprint planning and retrospective process. Together we review and improve our processes.
  • 360° feedback – multiple times a year we do 360 feedback within the team to ensure good communication & effective team work
  • Annual Summits & Dev Camps – Twice a year we meet in person for building team spirit and vision
  • Code quality – we care about the code we write, we use code reviews and shared standards
  • Modern technologies – we use a modern web technology stack.
  • Learning Environment – as a team we support each team member’s learning, always on the lookout for individual and team learning opportunities.
    • Course Fee Support – long-term team members are eligible for funding for relevant courses they take.
  • We work according to our values: Spirit, Professionalism & Communication
  • Weekly Watercooler meeting to talk freely and get to know your team members
  • Bi-Weekly Learning meetings to present & share what we are learning – great for supporting your learning and building portfolio! See our YouTube for recordings of some previous sessions
